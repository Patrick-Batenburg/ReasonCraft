# Rebuttals

A **rebuttal** answers an objection. When someone objects to your argument, a rebuttal shows why the objection does not hold.

## How to add one

You never choose "rebuttal" yourself: ReasonCraft works it out from where the card sits.

- Select a premise of an objection card.
- Press **Shift+Tab** (or use Home > Objection) to add an objection to it.
- Because this objection is aimed at an objection, it is shown as a rebuttal: orange, with the label "rebuts".

If you move the card somewhere else, its name follows: under a reason or under the claim it is an ordinary objection again.

## An example

Claim: "Our town should build a cycle path along the river."

- Objection: "A cycle path costs too much money."
- Rebuttal to that objection: "The province pays most of the cost."

The rebuttal says nothing new about the cycle path itself. It says that the objection is weaker than it looks.

## Rebuttals and the essay

In the Essay tab, each section lists the objections to its argument, followed by the rebuttals that answer them, so you can write how you deal with counterarguments. See [Outline and essay](outline-and-essay).

## Rating rebuttals

Rebuttals are rated like other cards: Strong, Weak or Nil on the Evaluate tab. A strong rebuttal shows "strongly rebuts". See [Rating your map](evaluation).
