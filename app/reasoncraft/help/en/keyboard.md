# Keyboard shortcuts

You can build, edit and rate a whole map without the mouse. The list at the bottom of this page shows the shortcuts of the computer you are using now.

## Keys on the map

These keys work when a box or card on the map is selected:

- **Tab** adds a reason, **Shift+Tab** an objection.
- **Enter** or **F2** edits the selected box; typing on a selected box starts editing it too.
- **Esc** stops editing. It also closes the find bar and the comments, and on the File page it goes back to the map.
- The **arrow keys** move the selection. Up from a premise selects its card; Down from a card selects its first premise. The arrows follow the layout direction of the map.
- On a Mac, **Backspace** deletes like Delete.
- **Ctrl+C**, **Ctrl+X** and **Ctrl+V** copy, cut and paste the selected item with everything under it.

## While you type in a box

Esc finishes, and Tab and Shift+Tab finish and add a reason or objection. The keys for a co-premise, a whole-card reason or objection, a source, saving, opening, a new map, finding and replacing (also F3), spelling, Search commands, switching or closing maps, F6 and F1 finish the box first and then do their work. All other keys are typed into the box.

## Moving around the window

- **F6** and **Shift+F6** move the keyboard focus to the next or previous part of the window: the ribbon, the Building panel, the map, the Text panel and the status bar.
- Once a control has the focus, Tab and Shift+Tab move between the controls, and Enter or Space presses a button.
- **F1** opens help about the part of the window you are in.
- **Ctrl+Tab** and **Ctrl+Shift+Tab** (or **Ctrl+PgDn** and **Ctrl+PgUp**) switch to the next or previous open map; **Ctrl+W** (or **Ctrl+F4**) closes it. These work in a text field too.
- The **Search commands** box in the title bar finds any command by name; pick one with the arrow keys and press Enter.

## On a Mac

Cmd takes the place of Ctrl and Option of Alt. Some keys differ: Replace is Option+Cmd+F and Search commands is Shift+Cmd+A, the rating keys use Option+Cmd with a digit, and switching maps uses Control (Control+Tab, Control+PgDn), as Cmd+Tab switches apps. The F-keys need the Fn key, unless your Mac is set to use them as standard function keys.
