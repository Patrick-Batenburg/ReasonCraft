# Exports and printing

Everything for handing in and sharing is on the **Export** tab of the ribbon. Exports run in the background: the status bar shows "Exporting…" and then "Export done." with an **Open folder** link.

## Pictures of the map

- **PNG**, **SVG** and **PDF** first ask what to export: the **Whole map** or the **Selection** (the selected item with everything under it), and in which colours: the **Light theme** (best for printing) or the **Current theme**.
- PNG: choose a scale from 1× to 4×, and a transparent background if you like. A PNG can be at most 16,384 pixels on a side; for a very large map, use SVG or PDF.
- SVG: text is turned into outlines by default, so the drawing looks the same on a computer without the font. Untick the option to keep text you can select.
- PDF: the text stays selectable. **Page setup…** sets the paper (A4 or Letter), the orientation, the margin and the fit: the whole map on one page, or spread across pages at a scale you choose.
- **Copy as image** puts a picture of the map on the clipboard, in the colours you see, to paste into another program.

Exports show what the map shows, without the parts that only make sense on screen: the selection, the **+** button, comment badges, link symbols, find highlights, the minimap and the dot grid. Items under Not connected are left out.

## Printing

**Print** makes a PDF with the page setup and opens it in your PDF viewer, where you print it.

## Text

- **Outline** saves the outline as Markdown, plain text or a Word document, with the numbers and switches of the Outline tab.
- **Essay** saves the essay as Markdown, plain text or a Word document, with the document's title and author and a heading for each part.
- **Argdown** saves the whole map as Argdown text, which other argument-mapping tools can read. See [Argdown](argdown).

Linking words and source names in these files follow the language of the document, not of the interface. Word files use the standard Title and Heading styles.
