# Outline and essay

A map helps you think; most assignments ask for text. The **Outline** and **Essay** tabs of the Text panel turn your map into writing. Open the panel with View > Text panel if it is hidden.

## The Outline tab

The Outline shows the whole map as a list, in the same order as the map:

- the claim first, then each card numbered `1`, `2`, … and the cards under them `1.1`, `1.2`, …;
- co-premises get letters: `1a`, `1b`;
- a card aimed at a whole card gets `.w1`, `.w2`;
- items under Not connected are numbered `P1`, `P2` at the end;
- sources sit unnumbered under their box.

The switches at the top turn on and off the **Numbers**, the **Labels** (linking words such as "because", "but" and "however"), **Indent**, **Colour** (each line in the colour of its role) and **Notes**. Your choice is remembered.

Click a line to select its box on the map. **Copy all** puts the outline on the clipboard as plain text, ready to paste into your own document. View > Numbers puts the same numbers on the boxes of the map.

## The Essay tab

The Essay tab is a writing plan that follows your map. It gives you a text field for each part:

- **Introduction**: shows the claim. Write the paragraph that introduces it.
- **One section per branch** under the claim, strongest first: strong cards, then unrated ones, then weak and then nil ones; with the same strength, reasons come before objections. A section lists its premises with their evidence, then the objections and the rebuttals that answer them, each with a field for its paragraph. The dots in a section's heading show the card's strength.
- **Conclusion**: shows the claim again. Sum up the main reasons.

Click a statement in the Essay tab to find it on the map. Typing in a field is one undo step, from the first key to the moment you leave the field.

The essay is saved inside the map's file. It is for reasoning maps; a grouping map has no essay, so there the Essay tab only says so and Export > Essay is greyed out.

## Text that has no place right now

- **Not in the essay right now** lists text you wrote for items that are now under Not connected or no longer part of an argument. It is kept, and comes back into its section when the item returns.
- **Unplaced notes**: when you delete an item that has essay text, the text moves here, labelled with the item's text, so nothing you wrote is lost. **Copy text** puts it on the clipboard and **Remove note** deletes it.

## Rate first, then write

The order of the sections comes from your ratings. Rate your cards before you write, and the strongest argument comes first. See [Rating your map](evaluation).

## Saving the text

Export > **Outline** and Export > **Essay** save the outline or the essay as Markdown, plain text or a Word document. See [Exports and printing](exports-and-print).
