# Snippets

You do not have to start with the claim. Sometimes it is easier to collect material first: quotes, figures and passages from what you read. The **Snippets** tab of the Text panel keeps that text beside your map, and you build the map from it. This is working bottom-up.

## Collecting text

- **New snippet** adds an empty snippet to type or paste into.
- **Paste as snippet** adds the text on the clipboard as a new snippet. Ctrl+V does the same while the pointer is over the tab and no text field has the focus.
- A snippet holds up to 50,000 characters; longer text is cut, with a notice.
- Drag a snippet's handle to change the order of the list.

Snippets are saved with the map.

## Tagging the source

Each snippet can say where its text comes from: a **Source type**, a **Citation** (author, year, title) and a **Source link**. Fill these in once, and every box you make from the snippet gets that source.

## From snippet to map

- Select a few words or a sentence in a snippet. A **Drag to map** chip appears: drag it onto the map.
- Or hold Alt and drag the snippet's handle to take its whole text.

Where you drop it decides what you get:

- on a claim or premise: a new reason under it;
- on a card: a reason for the whole card;
- on a category: a new category;
- on empty canvas: a reason under Not connected, to place later.

The snippet's source goes under the new premise, and the drop is one undo step. To make the new card an objection, use Home > **Type** afterwards. Notes, sources and empty text refuse the drop and show red.
