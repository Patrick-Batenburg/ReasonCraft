# Checks

The **Check** tab of the Text panel lists hints about your map, based on common rules for good arguments. The status bar shows how many there are; click that number to open the tab. Click a hint to select its item and bring it into view.

Checks are hints only: they never stop you from doing anything. Hover over a check's heading to read its rule, and click the **?** next to it to open this page.

## The key-word check

This is the most useful check, also known as the **key-word rule**: every key word of a statement should come back in the premises of the card under it. If a word of the claim appears in none of the premises, the premises do not yet explain it, and a co-premise is probably missing.

An example. Claim: "Schools should forbid phones in class." Reason: "Phones distract students." The check reports the missing key words **schools**, **forbid** and **class**: nothing in the reason says that schools should forbid what distracts students. Add a co-premise such as "Schools should forbid what distracts students in class", and the hint goes away. See [Co-premises](co-premises).

How the words are compared:

- Short words (fewer than four letters) and common words such as "the", "should" or "with" do not count.
- Close forms count as the same word, such as "robot" and "robots". Short words in singular and plural can still be reported when they are covered.
- The check looks at reasons and objections aimed at a claim or premise, not at cards aimed at a whole card.
- It uses the language of the document (English or Dutch). See [Languages and spelling](languages).

## The other checks

- **Two statements in one**: a claim or premise contains "and" or "or" ("en" or "of" in a Dutch map). If you could accept one part and reject the other, split it into two boxes. Words in quotation marks are not looked at.
- **Questions**: a claim or premise ends with a question mark. Write it as a statement you can accept or reject.
- **Nothing supports it**: a premise has no source and no reason under it. An objection does not count as support.
- **Empty categories**: in a grouping map, a main category has nothing under it. See [Grouping maps](grouping-maps).
- **Not rated yet**: you have started rating, but some statements, cards or sources have no rating yet. This is one hint with the number of unrated items; click it to go to the next one.
- **Ratings that disagree**: a card is rated strong while all its premises are rejected; a source is rated solid while its premise is rejected; or a card is rated nil while its premises are accepted and nothing objects to it.
- **In the parking area**: an item is still under Not connected, so it is not part of the argument yet. Notes there are left alone, and items under Not connected are treated as drafts: they are not asked for support or a rating.

## Ignoring a hint

Sometimes a hint does not apply. Click **Ignore** on it: the hint stays away for this map, and is saved with it. **Show ignored hints** at the bottom of the tab brings them all back.

## Turning checks off

File > Settings > Checks has a switch for each check. A check you turn off disappears from the tab and from the count in the status bar, for every map.
