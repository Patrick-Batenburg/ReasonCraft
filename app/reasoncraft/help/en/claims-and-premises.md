# Claims and premises

Every map is about one **claim**: the statement you want to argue for or examine. It sits in the box at the top of the map. A map has exactly one claim. If you have a second claim, start a second map, or keep it under **Not connected** for now.

A **premise** is a statement that gives a reason to believe something else, or to doubt it. Premises sit inside a card under the box they are about. When you press Tab on the claim, ReasonCraft makes a new card with one empty premise for you to fill in.

## Write statements, not topics

Claims and premises are statements: sentences that can be true or false, so you can accept or reject them.

- Good: "Homework helps students remember what they learned."
- Not a statement: "Homework" (a topic) or "Does homework help?" (a question).

The Check tab points out claims and premises written as questions. See [Checks](checks).

## One idea per box

A box should say one thing. "Cycling is healthy and cheap" holds two statements: someone could agree that it is healthy but not that it is cheap. Split it into two premises, so each can be supported and rated on its own. The Check tab warns about statements joined with "and" or "or".

## Kinds of boxes

- **Claim**: the statement at the top.
- **Premise**: a statement inside a card, giving a reason for or against what the card points at.
- **Source**: the evidence under a premise or the claim, such as a publication, a statistic or an expert. See [Sources](sources).
- **Category**: a box in a grouping map, which sorts a topic instead of arguing about it. See [Grouping maps](grouping-maps).
- **Note**: a sticky note on a box, for remarks to yourself. Notes are not part of the argument. Add one with Home > Note.

## Editing boxes

- Select a box and start typing, or press Enter or F2, to edit its text. Esc finishes.
- Text wraps by itself and the box grows to fit it.
- The Home tab's **Box** group gives a box a web link (Link), puts a picture in it (Image) or takes the picture out again (Remove image). A link opens only when you click its small link symbol or use Insert > **Open link** (Ctrl+Shift+L).
- Delete removes the selected item; what was under it moves to Not connected. Shift+Delete removes it with everything under it.
- Ctrl+C (Copy) and Ctrl+X (Cut) take the selected box or card with everything under it; Ctrl+V (Paste) puts a copy under the selected box or card, or in Not connected when nothing is selected, also in another map. A copied premise comes back as the premise of a new card. Other programs get the text, one box per line, and text copied in another program is pasted as a new box. The buttons are in the Home tab's **Clipboard** group.
- Drag a box between its neighbours to change their order, or press Alt+Left and Alt+Right.
